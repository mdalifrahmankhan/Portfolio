HOW I THINK

Understand the problem.
Then choose the tool.

I’m interested in the point where technology, information and communication meet. I’ve worked with software and digital systems, supported training and administrative work, handled records and accounts-related tasks, and created visual content.

I enjoy learning by tracing things back to how they work. That habit helps me move between different tools and disciplines while staying focused on the actual outcome, not just the software used to get there.

My academic background is in Economics, which gave me another way to think about information, systems, incentives and decisions. Alongside that, I have completed NSDA Level 3 training in Computer Operation and Graphic Design.

I value work that is clear, accurate, useful and thoughtfully finished. Whether the task involves a spreadsheet, a software workflow, a technical issue or a visual asset, I aim to make the result easier to understand and use.
